About the Company

Building Akure's Future
Since Day One

Nature's Beauty Construction is a proud division of Jenyo. Akarakiri Global Enterprise, founded and directed by Jenyo Akarakiri. With over three decades of hands-on contracting experience, the company has become one of the most trusted names in construction across Akure and the wider Ondo State region.

We specialise in two core disciplines — Structural Construction and Landscape Design — delivering projects that are built to last, crafted with precision, and shaped to inspire. Every project carries our founder's commitment to quality, integrity, and community.
