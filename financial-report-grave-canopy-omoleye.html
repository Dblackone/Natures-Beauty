Executive Summary

This report presents a complete financial account of the works executed and the expenditure incurred by Nature’s Beauty Construction on behalf of Justice Omoleye up to the date of issue. The records comprise three entirely independent expense groups, which have been kept separate throughout this report and in the Detailed Financial Breakdown:

  • Section A — Grave Canopy Construction Project at Ikole, executed in five distinct construction phases (foundation to roof level, roofing, block work, POP & ceiling, and tiling), with a cumulative expenditure of ₦6,186,000.
  • Section B — Ikole House Works, being separate works carried out at the client’s residence, totalling ₦313,000.
  • Section C — Other Client Expenses, being operational disbursements made on the client’s behalf and unrelated to the two construction streams, totalling ₦725,000.

The total expenditure incurred to date across all three groups is ₦7,224,000. Each phase and section total presented in this summary reconciles exactly with a detailed, itemized schedule in the Detailed Financial Breakdown, which has been transcribed faithfully from the original handwritten site expense log to serve as the financial audit trail.

At a glance
All five phases of the Grave Canopy Project have been executed and fully funded, placing the canopy at an advanced stage of completion. The Ikole House works and the sundry client disbursements have likewise been executed as recorded. No figure in this report has been estimated or invented; every value derives from the site log.

Scope of Works

A · Grave Canopy Construction Project — Ikole

The construction of a reinforced, roofed grave canopy, executed across five phases: (1) all structural works from site preparation and foundation up to parapet level — excavation, sand and granite, column and ring-beam reinforcement, cement, formwork and bricklaying labour to roof level; (2) roofing — roof timber, roofing sheets and accessories, and fixing labour; (3) block work — walling, parapet, plaster, sand and cement; (4) POP & ceiling — POP materials, screeding and finishing labour; and (5) tiling — wall and floor tiling with associated materials and labour.

B · Ikole House Works

Separate works executed at the client’s residence at Ikole, comprising fumigation of the property and plumbing works (supply of plumbing materials and plumber’s labour).

C · Other Client Expenses

Operational disbursements made on the client’s behalf, unrelated to the two construction streams, presented in two periods: earlier expenses (January–April) covering security-men salaries and a pressure-pump repair; and current miscellaneous expenses (May–present) covering a wreath and transportation, security salaries, and site logistics.
