Section 1

Executive Summary

This report presents a complete financial account of the works executed and the expenditure incurred by Nature’s Beauty Construction on behalf of Justice Omoleye up to the date of issue. The records comprise three entirely independent expense groups, which have been kept separate throughout this report and in the supporting appendices:

  • Section A — Grave Canopy Construction Project at Ikole, executed in five distinct construction phases (foundation to roof level, roofing, block work, POP & ceiling, and tiling), with a cumulative expenditure of ₦6,186,000.
  • Section B — Ikole House Works, being separate works carried out at the client’s residence, totalling ₦313,000.
  • Section C — Other Client Expenses, being operational disbursements made on the client’s behalf and unrelated to the two construction streams, totalling ₦725,000.

The total expenditure incurred to date across all three groups is ₦7,224,000. Each phase and section total presented in the main body reconciles exactly with a detailed, itemized schedule at Appendices A–H, which have been transcribed faithfully from the original handwritten site expense log to serve as the financial audit trail.

At a glance
All five phases of the Grave Canopy Project have been executed and fully funded, placing the canopy at an advanced stage of completion. The Ikole House works and the sundry client disbursements have likewise been executed as recorded. No figure in this report has been estimated or invented; every value derives from the site log.
Section 2

Project Background

Nature’s Beauty Construction was engaged by Justice Omoleye to construct a roofed grave canopy (the “Baba grave”) at Ikole-Ekiti. From commencement, the works progressed sequentially from setting-out and foundation, through the reinforced concrete structure and parapet, to roofing, walling, plaster and POP finishing, and finally tiling.

In parallel with the canopy, the contractor executed a number of discrete works at the client’s Ikole residence, and handled various operational disbursements on the client’s behalf over the period. Because these three streams are unrelated in purpose and funding, they are reported here as independent groups and are never merged into a single figure.

Section 4

Scope of Works

A · Grave Canopy Construction Project — Ikole

The construction of a reinforced, roofed grave canopy, executed across five phases: (1) all structural works from site preparation and foundation up to parapet level — excavation, sand and granite, column and ring-beam reinforcement, cement, formwork and bricklaying labour to roof level; (2) roofing — roof timber, roofing sheets and accessories, and fixing labour; (3) block work — walling, parapet, plaster, sand and cement; (4) POP & ceiling — POP materials, screeding and finishing labour; and (5) tiling — wall and floor tiling with associated materials and labour.

B · Ikole House Works

Separate works executed at the client’s residence at Ikole, comprising fumigation of the property and plumbing works (supply of plumbing materials and plumber’s labour).

C · Other Client Expenses

Operational disbursements made on the client’s behalf, unrelated to the two construction streams, presented in two periods: earlier expenses (January–April) covering security-men salaries and a pressure-pump repair; and current miscellaneous expenses (May–present) covering a wreath and transportation, security salaries, and site logistics.

Basis of preparation
The figures throughout are drawn directly from the contractor’s handwritten site expense log. Original order, dates, descriptions, quantities, unit rates and monetary values have been preserved; only spelling, punctuation and formatting have been improved. Where an original entry was abbreviated or partially unclear, it is retained faithfully and marked “as recorded in the original site expense log.”

Section A — Grave Canopy Project

The Grave Canopy Project was executed in five independent construction phases. Each phase is presented below with a short narrative and its financial summary, cross-referenced to its supporting appendix. The complete itemized schedules appear at Appendices A–E.

Phase 1 — Foundation to Roofing Level

The title “Foundation to Roofing Level” does not describe a single activity. It represents every construction activity executed from commencement on site up to the completion of the parapet, immediately before roofing began. It therefore includes site preparation and setting-out, excavation and digging of the foundation, the supply of sand and granite, the full reinforcement package for the columns and ring beam (16 mm, 12 mm and 8 mm bars, binding wire and welding / iron-bending labour), cement, formwork timber and bamboo props, carpentry labour, transportation, fuel, and the bricklaying labour to cast the structure through to roof level. The figure of ₦2,474,000 is accordingly the cumulative expenditure for all structural works up to parapet level and must not be read as one line item.

Section 8

Project Observations

  • All five construction phases of the grave canopy — foundation to roof level, roofing, block work, POP & ceiling and tiling — have been executed and fully funded, indicating that the primary construction of the canopy is at an advanced stage of completion.
  • The three expense streams are financially independent and have been kept separate throughout, both in the site records and in this report; no figures from different streams have been merged.
  • Recurring fuel and transportation entries reflect the distance between the contractor’s operational base and the Ikole and Ado sites, together with the several material-sourcing trips (for example, the booking of granite at Ado).
  • Expenditure was recorded contemporaneously in a handwritten site expense log. Every summary figure in this report is traceable to that log through Appendices A–H, and each appendix subtotal reconciles exactly with the corresponding figure in the main report.
  • A small number of entries in the original log were abbreviated. Where a description was not fully legible it has been retained faithfully and annotated “as recorded in the original site expense log”; no monetary value has been altered, estimated or invented.
On the treatment of Phase 1
The client’s attention is drawn to the fact that the Phase 1 figure of ₦2,474,000 is a cumulative total covering every structural activity from foundation up to parapet level. It is presented in full, line by line, at Appendix A, and is deliberately not compressed into a single line item.
