INTRODUCTION
Choosing the right finish

This catalogue presents the tile options Nature’s Beauty Construction recommends for wall rendering and finishing across our residential and commercial projects. It is designed to help you compare finishes side by side — by material, size, origin, quality grade and estimated cost — so that a confident selection can be made before works begin.

Our approach to material selection

We specify materials on the balance of durability, finish quality and value. Every option here is chosen for its performance in the Nigerian climate and for reliable local availability — whether an imported luxury range or a cost-effective locally produced line.

How to use this catalogue

Browse the four origin sections, then use the comparison table to shortlist. Record your choices in the Client Selection section on the final page. Our team will confirm final pricing and supply a physical sample of each shortlisted tile before approval.
