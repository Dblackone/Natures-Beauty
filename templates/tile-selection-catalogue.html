INTRODUCTION
Choosing the right finish

This catalogue presents the wall-cladding tiles Nature’s Beauty Construction supplies and installs for wall rendering — split-face “culture stone”, brick-effect and flagstone finishes for feature walls, façades, pillars and boundary walls. Compare each option by finish, size, origin, quality grade and estimated cost so a confident selection can be made before works begin.

Our approach to material selection

We specify cladding on the balance of durability, texture and value. Every option here is a hard-wearing porcelain or cement-composite finish chosen to perform in the Nigerian climate — imported Spanish and Chinese ranges held in ready supply.

How to use this catalogue

Browse the Spanish and Chinese sections, then use the comparison table to shortlist. Record your choices in the Client Selection section on the final page. Our team will confirm final pricing and provide a physical sample of each shortlisted tile before approval.
