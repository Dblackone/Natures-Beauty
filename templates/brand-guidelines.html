Nature’s Beauty CONSTRUCTION

Brand Guidelines

The identity system for a family-owned landscape construction company — rooted in Akure, building beautiful grounds across Nigeria.

01 — BRAND

Grounds people are proud of

Nature’s Beauty Construction designs, builds and maintains landscapes — gardens, grounds, hardscapes and green infrastructure — for homes, businesses and public institutions. We are family-owned, hands-on, and we treat every site like our own backyard.

Friendly

We speak plainly and warmly, like a trusted neighbour — never corporate or cold.

Grounded

Solid, geometric, dependable. Our work is built to last and our identity looks it.

Growing

From Akure to anywhere in Nigeria — living landscapes that get better with time.

02 — LOGO

The leaf lockup

A geometric double leaf — gold behind green — beside the wordmark. Keep clear space equal to the mark’s width on all sides. Never stretch, recolor outside the palette, or set the wordmark in another typeface.

03 — COLOR

Forest, gold and paper

Forest green leads. Harvest gold is the accent — badges, underlines, the sub-wordmark — never large fills. Paper and ink do the everyday work.

04 — TYPE

Sora & Work Sans

Sora
Aa Bb Cc 0123

Display & headings. Geometric and solid, but with rounded warmth. Weights 600–800. Sub-labels letterspaced 0.2–0.32em.

Work Sans
Aa Bb Cc 0123

Body, forms and captions. Weights 400–600. Body 16–17px at 1.6–1.7 line height; never below 12pt in print.

05 — VOICE

Like a neighbour who knows the land

Say “We’ll walk your site with you and give you a plain price.”
Not “We leverage end-to-end landscaping solutions to maximize outdoor value.”
Always First person plural, active voice, specific plants and materials, real timelines.
