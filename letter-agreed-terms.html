Agreed Terms and Path Forward — [Project Name / Site Location]

Dear Sir / Madam,

Thank you for the opportunity to quote for your project and for the engagement so far. Following our site visit, we reached an agreement at sixteen thousand naira (₦16,000) per square metre, against which an advance of ₦2,000,000 was received. You have since reached out to revise that rate to fourteen thousand naira (₦14,000) per square metre, on the basis of an alternative quotation from your quantity surveyor.

We would like to propose a clear and fair way forward.

First, before any further discussion of figures, we intend to refund the advance of ₦2,000,000 to you in full. We feel this is in the best interest of both parties. With a payment already sitting with us, we would prefer not to be in any position where it could appear that the company’s interest is simply in holding onto funds. Returning the advance clears the air entirely and lets us approach the matter on a clean footing, with no financial obligation hanging over the conversation.

Once the refund is settled, we would be glad to come back to the table and renegotiate the terms openly, so that whatever is agreed is something both sides are genuinely comfortable with.

That said, we would be remiss not to raise one point honestly, in the spirit of a good working relationship. Having reached an initial agreement and then returned to renegotiate on the strength of an outside quotation does, candidly, raise a concern on our side. It is the kind of early signal that can point to friction further down the line, regardless of the terms eventually settled. We mention this not as a grievance, but because we believe an honest start is the best foundation for the work—and we would rather address it now than leave it unspoken.

We value the prospect of working with you and look forward to your thoughts.
