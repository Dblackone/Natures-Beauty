Project Description Installation of Interlocking Pavement & Plaster Rendering Works
Scope of Work

Laying of interlocking paving stones (paving stones to be provided by the client) at the rate of ₦2,500 per square metre. The rate is inclusive of clearing of debris and obstructions, scarifying, and levelling of the base ahead of laying. The quoted area of 100 sqm is provisional; the final area laid will be jointly measured on completion and billed at the agreed rate per square metre. The works also include plaster rendering over the septic tanks and inspection chamber at a lump sum.
